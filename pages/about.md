## About the Exhibition

This virtual gallery presents a selection of works from *Art, Movement, and Disability: Transcending the Beauty Paradigm*, on view at the [Ewing Gallery of Art + Architecture](https://ewing-gallery.utk.edu/) at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville (1715 Volunteer Blvd.) from August 26 to October 18, 2026.

The exhibition brings into the gallery the 2026 book of the same name by Dr. Timothy Hiles, Associate Professor Emeritus of Art History at the University of Tennessee. It shows a selection of the artists featured in his research, whose work challenges conventional narratives of disability, embodiment, and representation.
The exhibition is organized around three themes: flipping the script (reclamation), invisibility, and the conversation.

## The Artists

The works collected here are by three of the exhibiting artists:

## Accessibility

Because this exhibition is about disability and representation, the site aims to be usable by everyone.
Each image has a written visual description (alt text) that screen readers read aloud, in addition to the catalog description shown on each item page.
If you find something that is hard to use, please [open an issue]({{ site.source-code }}/issues).
